# 🏓 PWONYX
**Classic arcade chaos. Pure momentum. Electrifying beats.**

> *PWONYX is an ongoing tribute to the golden age of high-speed web arcade games, reimagining the hypnotic, frantic action of the cult classic Pwong 2.*

---

## 🌌 Overview

**PWONYX** is an adrenaline-fueled arcade tennis experience where reaction time, rhythm, and tactical defense collide. 

Unlike traditional paddle games that test your patience with a single slow ball, PWONYX escalates into a frantic swarm of up to **20 simultaneous balls**, streaking glowing neon trails across an infinite cosmic arena. Powered by a dynamic, multi-layered soundtrack that builds intensity as the chaos rises, every rally feels like a pulse-pounding rhythm duel.

---

## ⚡ Core Gameplay & Features

### 🎾 The Ball Swarm
- **Escalating Chaos:** Matches begin with a single duel and rapidly multiply up to **20 simultaneous balls** bouncing at blistering speeds.
- **Inverse Penalty Scaling:** Staying true to the spirit of classic arcade gameplay, the more balls currently active on screen, the less an individual missed ball penalizes your score or health. High-risk saves and bold defensive clears are always rewarded.

### 🌀 Magnus Spin & Curve Shots
- Slicing your paddle horizontally while striking a ball applies **Magnus Spin**, curving the ball's trajectory mid-flight to slip past the opponent's guard. Successful curved goals grant substantial bonus points and visual flare.

### 👻 Ghost Paddles & Tactical Ammo
- Discharged with a single click or keystroke, **Ghost Paddles** launch forward across the court as temporary secondary barriers, intercepting incoming balls before they reach your baseline.
- Collect falling power-up orbs to replenish your ghost ammunition during intense volleys.

### 💎 Falling Power-Ups & Buffs
- **Wide Paddle:** Extends defensive coverage across the baseline.
- **Slow Motion:** Drops game velocity while applying an atmospheric low-pass filter to the audio, giving you precious seconds to regain control.
- **Sticky / Magnet:** Attracts incoming downward balls toward your paddle center.
- **Overdrive:** Doubles point multipliers and supercharges return velocity.

### 🌀 Hypnotic Visual Ambiance
- **Balanced 4:3 Arena:** The physical playfield maintains a focused, balanced 4:3 ratio centered on your screen, preserving fair reaction times and natural mouse coverage across all display sizes (widescreen, ultrawide, or mobile) with zero black bars.
- **Infinite Cosmic Background:** Procedural mountain contours, cosmic nebulae, and audio-reactive laser rails frame the action seamlessly.
- **Glowing Persistence Trails:** Balls leave fluid comets of incandescent light across the arena, creating an authentic retro arcade signature.

## 🏆 Progression & Game Modes

### Game Modes
- **Casual / Medium:** Balanced ball speeds and gradual swarm escalation.
- **Intense / Insane:** Brutal ball acceleration, swift AI reaction, and swarms reaching 20 balls in seconds.
- **Survival:** Defend a draining health bar against endless waves. Each miss damages your core; each score restores vital energy.
- **2-Player Local Versus:** Battle a friend on the same screen with dedicated baseline and top-court controls.

### Upgrades & Achievements
- Earn credits through skillful rallies, curve goals, and high match domination ratings.
- Upgrade your paddle extension, spin strength, power-up duration, and ghost paddle durability.
- Track 12 classic arcade achievements, from speed marathons to the prestigious *97% Domination Club*.

## 🎵 Dynamic Adaptive Music

Music is at the very heart of PWONYX. 

The soundtrack is split into synchronized musical layers (Drums, Bass, Harmony, and Lead). As the number of balls on screen multiplies, the audio automatically swells in real time:
- **1–3 Balls:** Tight, rhythmic groove (Drums & Bass).
- **4–7 Balls:** Melodic synths and arpeggios kick in.
- **8+ Balls:** Full overdrive with screaming leads and soaring melodies.
- **Pause & Slow-Mo:** Sweeps dynamic low-pass filters for an instant underwater feel.

Players can also load their own multi-stem audio creations directly through the in-game music studio.

## 🎮 How to Play & Controls

### Controls
- **Move Paddle:** Mouse movement (or `A` / `D` / `Arrow Keys`).
- **Launch Ghost Paddle:** Left Click, `Spacebar`, or `W`.
- **2-Player Versus:**
  - **Player 1 (Bottom):** Mouse or `A` / `D` + `W` / `Space`.
  - **Player 2 (Top):** `Left Arrow` / `Right Arrow` + `Down Arrow` / `Enter`.
- **Quick Shortcuts:**
  - `F`: Toggle Fullscreen.
  - `P` or `Esc`: Pause / Resume.
  - `R`: Restart match.
  - `M`: Mute / Unmute.
